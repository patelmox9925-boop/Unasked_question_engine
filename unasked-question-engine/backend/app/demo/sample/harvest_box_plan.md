# Harvest Box: Launch Plan

## Overview
Harvest Box is a subscription meal-kit web app for busy households. We launch publicly in March with two engineers and one part-time designer.

## Timeline
Beta opens on 1 February. Public launch is 15 March. The warehouse integration will be finished before launch.

## Access and accounts
Staff log in to the admin console with a shared password kept in the team spreadsheet. Customers sign up with an email address and pay by card.

## Budget
Hosting budget is capped at 500 dollars per month. We expect strong growth after launch, with a target of 20,000 subscribers within six months.

## Payments
Card payments go through our own checkout form, and card details are stored in our database so that renewals are quick.

## Fulfilment
Orders sync with a legacy warehouse system through a nightly CSV export. The warehouse ships nationwide. Every order is promised delivery within 24 hours.

## Support
Customer support will be handled by the founders. No support hours have been defined.

## Data
We will collect dietary preferences and allergy information to personalise each box.
