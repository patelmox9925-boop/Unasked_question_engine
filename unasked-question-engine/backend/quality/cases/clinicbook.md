# ClinicBook: Appointment Booking for Dental Clinics

## Goal
ClinicBook lets patients of a 12-clinic dental group book and cancel appointments online. The goal is to cut
phone calls to reception by 60 percent within three months of launch.

## Scope
The first release covers online booking, SMS reminders and a staff calendar. Online payment and insurance
checks are planned for a later release.

## Team and schedule
One contractor will build the system in 8 weeks. Go-live is the first Monday of next month for all 12 clinics
at the same time.

## Data
Patient names, phone numbers and treatment types are stored. The calendar data from the existing system will
be imported on the day before go-live.

## Reminders
SMS reminders are sent 24 hours before each appointment through a bulk SMS provider. Each clinic sends about
400 reminders a day.

## Cancellations
Patients can cancel online at any time. Receptionists confirm every cancellation by phone.

## Support
Reception staff will answer patient questions about the website.
